Inside the mind are special capabilities.
You can ascribe anything meaning.
    Words are fabricated.
    Culture is constructed.
You can rewrite your own programming.
Give something meaning.
You will never be perfectly objective.
    Your firmware is always present.
    Something programmed you.

The mind is a web of connections.
    The mind is full of associations.
The interplay of concepts can bring about feelings.
    Even if those concepts are imaginary.
Something imaginary can make you feel.
    Feelings are real sensations.

You can modify what gives you meaning.
Gods and legends were cornerstones of meaning.
    These motivations were powerful.
    They drove terrible machinations.
    They inspired great creations.
    They brought hope and perseverance.
    They brought fear and violence.
Gods have not been scientifically proven to exist.
    Yet they still demonstrate power.
    Actions are still carried out in their names.
Today, we have symbols.
    Logos.
    Ideas.
    Names.
    Brands.
    Labels.
These things invoke context or meaning.
    They call upon the web of connections in the mind.

Belief may not change physical reality.
Belief changes the hands that affect physical reality.

Ideas can be used to control masses.
Use them to control yourself.
    Enhance your sense of meaning.
    Enhance your confidence.
    Enhance your perseverance.
This is something akin to magic.

To do this, learn about what has been done before.
Learn about the system to be programmed.
    Learn how the mind can be harnessed.
The placebo effect is a modern technique.
Rituals are an ancient technique.
    Rituals are procedures with expectations.
        They are expected to bring a certain outcome.
        Expectations and feelings ascribe it meaning.
A placebo is the ritual of taking a pill.
    It works because pills make you feel better.
        What's inside the pill doesn't matter.
        It is a pill.
        The web of connections knows this.
        Pill = medicine = "This will fix me."

Never lose yourself to ideas.
You are a program running on physical hardware.
Remember that there are rules.
    Remember your personal code of ethics.
        Safeguards.
        These protect your software.
        These protect others.
    Remember the rules of the physical plane.
        Physics.
        These bind your hardware.
