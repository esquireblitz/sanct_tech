Humans Behind the Screen
528
2026/07/08

Behind a screen is often a human.
Be kind when you interact with them.
They are as real as you are.
    The screen is a filter.
    The screen adds context to things inside.
Anything inside of the screen feels distant.
    The screen is like a window.
    There is a barrier of glass.
Some things through the screen are fake.
Some things through the screen are real.
There is a filter.
    Reality can be warped by the window.
    A perspective applied to you view.
    Who put the things on the screen?

Be aware of the filter.
Remember what is real.
Do not let the filter diminish you.
    Do not forget the reality of others.
        Treat others with decency.
    Do not accept all as reality.
        The window can be warped.
