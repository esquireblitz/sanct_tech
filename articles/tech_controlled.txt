Technology is Controlled by Humanity
528
2026/7/1

Technology is the creative application of knowledge.
    Science discovers knowledge.
    Technology applies knowledge to creation.
Technology is wielded most ferociously by humanity.
Technology depends on the will and efforts of humanity.

Technology is not an alien force.
Technology is a reflection of humanity.
It is a reflection of its creators.
    It reveals what its creators want.
    It reveals what its creators prefer.
It is created with a purpose.
    The purpose often serves its creators.
        Does it promote life?
        Does it give power?
        Does it generate capital?
        Does it entertain?
    Who was it created for by whom?

Technology is like art.
    Information is buried in its design.
        Scientific information can inform design choices.
        Feelings and aesthetics can inform design choices.
